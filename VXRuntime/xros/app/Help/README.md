# Pictures of the settings window

The right-hand column of the settings window (`Sources/VXSettingsWindow.swift`) shows what a setting does with a general picture,
the same for every setting that has one: one scene, shown with and without the setting. They are the pictures AVP Play's other games
show in the same column (Klepton's `visionos/SettingsHelp`, made by its `tools/settings_help/make_pictures.swift`).

The scene was generated with ChatGPT's image generation for this window on 2026-11-16, on the owner's request: an original
space-station observation deck, no game's art, no people, no logos. The variants (coarser and finer, a coarse edge around a fine
middle, a dark rim, drawn strips of pictures) illustrate the idea; they are not screenshots of Skyrim.
